# GLOSSARY.md Format

`GLOSSARY.md` is the canonical language for a teaching workspace. All explainers, exercises, and learning records should adhere to it. Compressing a concept into a tight definition is evidence of understanding.

## Structure

```md
# {Topic} Glossary

{One or two sentences describing the topic covered.}

## Terms

**Hypertrophy**:
Muscle growth driven by mechanical tension and metabolic stress over repeated training sessions.
_Avoid_: Bulking, getting big

**Progressive overload**:
Systematically increasing demand on a muscle over time through load, volume, or intensity.
_Avoid_: Pushing harder, levelling up

**RPE (Rate of Perceived Exertion)**:
A 1–10 self-rating of how hard a set felt, where 10 is failure and 8 means two repetitions remain.
_Avoid_: Effort score, intensity rating
```

## Rules

- Add a term only after the user can use it correctly. A glossary records compressed knowledge; it is not a dictionary for new concepts.
- Pick one preferred term when synonyms exist and list other terms as aliases to avoid.
- Keep definitions to one or two sentences. Define what a term is, not what it does or how to use it.
- Prefer glossary terms inside later definitions so the workspace keeps one language.
- Group terms under subheadings when natural clusters emerge; a flat list is fine when terms cohere.
- Resolve field ambiguity explicitly, for example: “In this workspace, ‘set’ always means a working set; warm-ups are tracked separately.”
- Revise definitions as understanding deepens; do not leave stale entries.
